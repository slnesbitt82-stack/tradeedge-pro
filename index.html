Field Reference
date Trade date. Accepted: YYYY-MM-DD   MM/DD/YYYY   DD-MM-YYYY. Can also contain a combined datetime: 2025-03-14 09:32 — the time is split out automatically.
time Entry time. Accepted: HH:MM   HH:MM:SS   9:30 AM. Optional — defaults to 09:30 if the column is absent or empty. Omit entirely if using a combined datetime in the date column.
direction Long or Short (case-insensitive). Also accepts Buy/Sell, B/S, L/S.
asset_class Futures   Forex   Stocks   Crypto   Options. Defaults to Stocks if blank.
contract_
multiplier Contract multiplier — e.g. 0.5 for MYM, 5 for MES, 100 for options contracts. Defaults to 1.
tags Pipe-separated: breakout|trend|fomo
fee Commission / fee in dollars. Subtracted from P&L when Include Commission in P&L is on in Settings.
option_type Options only. Call or Put.
strike Options only. Strike price, e.g. 450.
expiry Options only. Expiration date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
underlying Options only. Underlying ticker, e.g. SPY.
💡 Delimiter: Comma (,) and semicolon (;) are both auto-detected.
💡 Datetime in one column: If your broker exports 2025-03-14 09:32:00 as a single value, place it in the date column — the app splits it automatically.
💡 Quoted values: Values wrapped in double quotes (e.g. "my note") are handled correctly.

Everything you need to know to get the most out of your private trading journal. Use the sections below as a reference any time.
🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)
1
Set up your trading account
You are in Settings → Account → Trading Accounts right now. Click + Add Account, enter your starting balance, broker name, and account type (Live, Demo, or Prop). This is the foundation all P&L calculations build on. You can add multiple accounts and switch between them using the account picker at the top of the screen.
2
Log your first trade
Click the ➕ icon in the sidebar to open the 5-step trade wizard. Required: symbol, entry price, exit price. Optional but useful: quantity, contract multiplier, commission/fee, setup type, tags, and notes. P&L (net of fees) calculates automatically. You can also log trades directly from the Calendar by clicking any day → + Log Trade.
3
Use your checklists before every session
Open the 📅 Calendar, click today, then tap 🌅 Pre-Session to run your morning preparation checklist before your session begins, and 📋 Pre-Trade to validate each setup before you enter. Both checklists are fully customisable in the ✅ Checklist page — add, edit, delete, or reorder items to match your exact process.
4
Analyse your performance
The 📊 Dashboard shows your live balance and equity curve. The 📈 Analytics page breaks down P&L by time of day, day of week, and asset class to reveal exactly when and what you trade best — and worst.

📋 Pre-Trade — Setup validation checklist saved independently per day. Cyan progress bar.

💰 How P&L and Fees Work
All P&L figures — account balance, totals, win rate, equity curve, and every chart — are calculated as net P&L: gross trade profit/loss minus any commission or fee you enter on that trade.

To log a fee: when adding or editing a trade, enter the commission amount in the Commission / Fees ($) field on the Details tab. Each trade has its own fee field — useful for brokers that charge per lot or per side.

To toggle fee deduction: go to Settings → Trading → Include Commission in P&L. When off, all figures show gross P&L. The setting takes effect immediately and can be toggled at any time.
🔒 Privacy & Data Storage
TradeEdge Pro stores all data in your browser's localStorage — on your device, in your browser, with no server involved. Nothing is transmitted or stored externally. No one else can access your data.

⚠️ Clearing your browser's site data or cache will delete your trades. To protect your data, go to Settings → Data → Export Full Journal (JSON) and save the file regularly. You can restore it using Import JSON Backup on the same page.

Multiple users: each username is completely isolated — different traders on the same device cannot access each other's data. Passwords are hashed with SHA-256.

Install as PWA (Settings → Data → App Installation) for a more stable storage environment, offline access, and a native app experience on any device.

📋 Logging & Managing Trades
➕ Adding a Trade — 5-Step Wizard
Click the ➕ icon in the sidebar to open the wizard:
Step 1 Details — Symbol, asset class, direction (Long/Short), entry/exit prices, quantity, multiplier, date/time, session, and commission/fee.
Step 2 Setup — Select the setup type (e.g. Breakout, VWAP), market conditions, timeframe, and describe your edge for this trade.
Step 3 Tags — Tag the trade with emotion, strategy, or mistake labels for later filtering and analysis.
Step 4 Media — Attach chart screenshots or video links as evidence of your entry/exit.
Step 5 Notes — Write pre-trade thoughts, in-trade observations, and a post-trade review. Only symbol + entry + exit are required.
📋 Trade Log
The Trade Log (sidebar 📋 icon) lists every trade. Use the filter bar to narrow by date range, symbol, direction, setup type, win/loss, or tag. Click any row to expand it and see full notes, media, and setup details. Use the ✎ edit button to update any field.
📥 Importing from CSV
Go to Import CSV in the sidebar.
Required: date, time, symbol, direction, entry, exit
Optional: asset_class, quantity, contract_multiplier, fee, setup, tags, notes
Date formats: YYYY-MM-DD, MM/DD/YYYY, DD-MM-YYYY.
Time formats: HH:MM, HH:MM:SS, 9:30 AM. If your broker exports a combined datetime in one column (e.g. 2025-03-14 09:32), place it in the date column — the app splits it automatically.
Tags are pipe-separated: breakout|trend|fomo. Comma and semicolon delimiters both supported.
💰 Fees & Net P&L
Enter your broker commission in the Commission / Fees ($) field when adding or editing a trade. Every P&L figure in the app — balance, totals, win rate, equity curve — automatically subtracts fees. You can disable this globally in Settings → Trading → Include Commission in P&L.
📝 Missed Trades
In the sidebar, Missed Trades lets you log setups you saw but didn't take — with estimated P&L, the reason you skipped, and a lesson field. The Analytics tab shows your total missed opportunity over time and which setups you most often avoid.
📅 Using the Calendar
📆 Day Side Panel
Click any day to open the side panel. It shows the day's net P&L, trade count, checklist progress bars, and every trade logged. Each trade card has a ✎ Edit button (opens the full edit modal) and a ✕ Delete button. 🗑 Clear All removes all trades for that day at once.
🌅 Pre-Session Checklist
Run this before your trading session begins. Click a day → 🌅 Pre-Session. Work through your morning routine items (review economic calendar, identify S/R levels, assess emotional state, etc.). Items come from your Checklist page and are fully editable. Once saved, the side panel shows a green progress bar and count.
📋 Pre-Trade Checklist
Run this before entering each trade to validate your setup. Click a day → 📋 Pre-Trade. Confirm your setup matches strategy, stop-loss is defined, R:R is acceptable, and you're not trading out of FOMO or revenge. Saved independently from Pre-Session. Shows a cyan bar in the side panel.

📊 Calendar Stats Panel
Below the calendar grid you'll find a monthly summary (total trades, net P&L, win rate, profit factor, best day), a daily P&L sparkline, a day-of-week breakdown, and a weekly P&L bar — all updating live as you add trades.
📊 Reading Your Analytics
🏠 Dashboard KPIs
Balance = starting balance + all-time net P&L (fees deducted).
Total P&L = sum of all net trades across all time.
Profitability % = total net P&L ÷ starting balance × 100.
Win Rate = % of trades where net P&L > 0.
Profit Factor = gross winning P&L ÷ gross losing P&L. Above 1.5 is solid; above 2.0 is excellent.
Avg Win / Avg Loss — compare these to see if your winners outsize your losers.
📈 Equity Curve
Plots your running account balance after every single trade. A steady upward slope is the goal. Drawdown dips show when your approach changed — look at what was different in those stretches (time of day, setup type, emotional state) to identify patterns.
⏰ Time of Day Analysis
Found in Analytics → Charts. Shows net P&L aggregated by hour of day. If you consistently lose during a specific hour (e.g. 12:00–13:00 lunchtime chop), this chart makes it obvious. Consider avoiding those windows entirely.
📅 Day of Week Breakdown
Shows which days of the week generate the most P&L. Also visible on the calendar monthly stats panel. If Mondays or Fridays are consistently your worst days, the data will tell you — and you can act on it.
📦 Asset Class Breakdown
The Asset tab in Analytics shows P&L, win rate, and average trade result split by Futures, Forex, Stocks, and Crypto. If you're profitable in Futures but losing in Forex, you now have data to focus your effort.
💡 Key insight: The single most actionable use of analytics is finding the intersection of when and what you trade best. Look at time-of-day combined with your top setup types to find your highest-probability window.

🔒 Privacy, Data & Backup
Your data never leaves your device
TradeEdge Pro stores everything in your browser's localStorage. There is no server, no cloud sync, and no external connection. No one — including the developers — can access your journal. Your trades are yours alone.
⚠️ Back up your data regularly
Clearing your browser's cache or site data will delete your trades. This is the most common cause of data loss. To back up: Settings → Data → Export Full Journal (JSON). Save this file to your computer, cloud drive, or email it to yourself. To restore, use Import JSON Backup on the same page.
👥 Multiple Users on One Device
Each username has completely isolated data. Multiple traders can share a device — each sees only their own trades, settings, and checklists. Passwords are hashed with SHA-256 and never stored in plain text. To manage accounts: Settings → Account → Delete Account or Settings → Data → Delete All Profiles.
📱 Install as an App (PWA)
Install TradeEdge Pro to your home screen via Settings → Data → App Installation or click the install icon in your browser's address bar. This gives you: offline access, a more stable storage environment, and a native app experience on iOS, Android, and desktop. Recommended for daily use.
🔑 Changing or Recovering Your Password
Go to Settings → Account → Change Password. You'll need your current password to confirm. If you forget your password, there is no recovery — data is local only and there's no reset email. Write your password down somewhere safe.
